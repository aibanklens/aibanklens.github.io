Bank AI Index

Bank AI Index is an independent monitoring metric tracking AI risk exposure and control maturity across Canada's Big-6 commercial banks. Built on the OSFI-FCAC AI Risk Framework, it covers five years of data (2020–2025) across 9 risk categories — from model governance and data privacy to third-party AI and cyber resilience.
