Why I Built This

Banks are increasingly leveraging AI and cloud services to drive innovation, improve customer experience, and optimize operations. As these technologies become more deeply embedded in core financial services, their functionality and associated risks will have a growing impact on financial stability and institutional performance.

I built this index to systematically monitor and assess how Canada's Big-6 banks are navigating AI-related risks — from model governance and third-party dependencies to cybersecurity and regulatory compliance. I find this space genuinely fascinating, and I'm always eager to connect with professionals and researchers who share this interest.
